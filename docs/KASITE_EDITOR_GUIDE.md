# Editing the MamaMeds website

Open [MamaMeds Studio](https://mamamedsng.sanity.studio/) and sign in with your own Sanity account. Choose **Website Content**.

1. **Edit:** choose the relevant tab and update the text or images. Changes save as a draft.
2. **Preview:** open Presentation to see your draft. If it hasn't refreshed, use **Refresh preview** in the website's draft banner.
3. **Publish:** return to Website Content and click **Publish**. The website updates shortly afterwards. Preview changes are not public until you publish.

## Add gallery photos yourself

1. Open **Tobi’s story & gallery** → **Gallery — photos and videos**.
2. Choose **Add item → Gallery photo** and upload a photo from your device.
3. Fill in the image description (alt text), for example “MamaMeds volunteers supporting women at our outreach.” This is read aloud by screen readers; it isn’t shown on the page. The gallery has no captions.
4. Add more photos in the same way. Drag the items to arrange photos and videos together in one grid.
5. Preview, then Publish. You do not need Cloudinary or a developer for photos.

Use clear original photos. The site creates responsive image sizes and loads gallery photos as needed. Gallery tiles are cropped to the same shape so the grid stays tidy; clicking a tile opens the full, uncropped photo.

## Other images and video

- **Hero:** replace or remove the optional hero photo.
- **Our work:** upload an optional photo for each of the three cards.
- **Founder:** replace your portrait and edit biography paragraphs.
- **Gallery videos:** upload the video to Cloudinary, then add a video item and paste the **preview** link (small, plays silently in the grid), the **full-size** link (opens with sound when clicked) and the poster image link. Give it a short title, used only by screen readers, such as “Our second outreach”. Ask the developer for the links if you’re unsure which Cloudinary settings to use.
- **Tobi’s story:** the “In her words” label and intro line are text fields in the same tab; the video works like a gallery video, and its title (“Tobi’s story”) is shown under it.
- **Site settings:** update email, social links, search/share title, description, and sharing image. Social apps may take time to refresh an old preview.

Required fields show a message if anything needs fixing. Layout, colors, page order, and navigation destinations are maintained in code. If anything looks wrong, leave it as a draft and contact the developer before publishing.
